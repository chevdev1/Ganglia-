# Public disclaimer

Ganglia is a shared digital character. Its words are generated by software — a language model when one is configured, otherwise a local writer. Cached speech is the same text read aloud. Either way, a human did not grow this voice in a dish.

A node is the right to send public scenarios into one shared memory. It is not living tissue, not a share of a company, and not an investment.

Everything you send is published in the archive. Do not include names, addresses, or anything private.

Signals shown in the interface are software-generated unless a thought says otherwise. No hardware connection is inferred from a software payload.
